## Why every report is accepted with corrections

Each report is complete enough to preserve as evidence, but each mixes:

1. external facts and research findings;
2. target-specific inference;
3. candidate design recommendations.

Acceptance with corrections preserves sources, comparisons, negative evidence
and design options while preventing candidate schemas, thresholds, product
choices, storage patterns or policies from being mistaken for Owner-approved
target truth.

## MA-DR-11 short-runtime ruling

The Owner-reported five-minute runtime triggered enhanced review. The report
passed topic, repository binding, required outputs, source portability,
negative-evidence and load-bearing source checks. Its defects are scope and
wording limitations addressable by reviewer corrections. Runtime alone does
not justify a clean rerun.
